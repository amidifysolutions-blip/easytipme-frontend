Questions, answered.

Everything owners and teams ask before getting started.

Do customers need an app to tip? +
No. Customers scan your QR with their phone camera, pick a team member, choose an amount and pay, all in the browser. No app, no account, no cash.
How does my team get paid? +
Tips are paid out securely through Stripe. Each team member connects their bank once and receives their tips automatically on a schedule.
Is it free to start? +
Yes. You can register your business, add your team and start collecting tips for free. Optional Pro features are available for owners who want more.
Can tips be split fairly across the team? +
Yes. Owners can turn on tip pooling to split tips equally among the team members on shift, fairly and automatically.
Which businesses is it for? +
Restaurants, cafés, salons, barbershops, hotels, gyms & fitness studios, clinics, hospitals and any service team that gets tipped.
