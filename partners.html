Questions

Does it cost me anything to join?

No. Joining is free, there is nothing to buy, no subscription of your own, and no fee ever comes out of what you earn.

When and how do I get paid?

Monthly, once you have built up at least $10. Where Stripe is available your money goes straight to your own Stripe account; otherwise we pay by Wise, USDT or bank transfer.

What if someone opens my link but does not sign up straight away?

You still get the credit, as long as they come back and sign up within 90 days. Once they make their first payment, that venue is tied to you permanently.

Do I keep earning if I stop referring?

Yes — you never lose the venues you brought. But your rate steps down 7 percentage points after each 6 months without a new paying venue, down to a floor of 10%.

Can I refer my own venue?

No. Referring yourself is not allowed, and each venue can only ever be credited to one partner.

Is there a quota or an approval process?

Neither. There is no minimum number of referrals to hit and nobody has to approve you. You join, you accept the agreement, you get your link.
